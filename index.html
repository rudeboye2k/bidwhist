How to Play

The deal. 54 cards — a full deck plus both jokers. Everyone gets 12; six go face-down to the kitty. Twelve tricks (books) plus the kitty makes 13 books in every hand.

The auction. Starting left of the dealer, each player gets one call: pass, or bid a number from 3–7 — just the number — or that number No-Trump. A bid promises six plus the bid books: bid 4 means 10 books. A four is a four: to beat it, bid five, or four No-Trump.

The kitty. Whoever bids highest takes all six kitty cards, then names trump and picks Uptown (high cards win) or Downtown (after the ace, low cards win: A, 2, 3 … K). Won it in No-Trump? Just pick Uptown or Downtown. Then bury six — and that buried book belongs to the bidding team.

The play. The bidder leads. Follow suit if you can; the jokers are the two biggest trumps. In No-Trump the jokers are throw-offs — they can never win a book, you can’t lead one, and you only play one when you’re out of the led suit.

The score. Make the bid and the bidding team scores every book past six. Get set and you lose the bid instead. No-Trump doubles it, up or down. Defenders set a bid by winning 8 − bid books: four books stops a 4-bid, three stops a 5. Taking all 13 is a Boston. First team to the target wins; fall to minus-target and you lose.
