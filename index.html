    How to Play

    The deal. 54 cards — a full deck plus both jokers. Everyone gets 12; six go face-down to the kitty. Twelve tricks (books) plus the kitty makes 13 books in every hand.

    The auction. Starting left of the dealer, each player gets one call: pass, or bid 3–7 as Uptown (high cards win), Downtown (after the ace, low cards win: A, 2, 3 … K), or No-Trump. A bid promises six plus the bid books — bid 4 means 10 books. At the same number, No-Trump outranks Uptown and Downtown; Uptown and Downtown tie, so you must go up a number to beat one with the other.

    The kitty. The high bidder names trump (or picks high/low for No-Trump), takes all six kitty cards, and buries six — and that buried book belongs to the bidding team.

    The play. The bidder leads. Follow suit if you can; the jokers are the two biggest trumps. In No-Trump the jokers are dead cards — they can never win a book and only fall when you’re out of the led suit.

    The score. Make the bid and the bidding team scores every book past six. Get set and you lose the bid instead. No-Trump doubles it, up or down. Defenders set a bid by winning 8 − bid books: four books stops a 4-bid, three stops a 5. Taking all 13 is a Boston. First team to the target wins; fall to minus-target and you lose.
